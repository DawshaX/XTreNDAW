# Privacy Policy — XDAW NOVA

XDAW NOVA is a personal content-automation project (religious/educational short videos).

- The automation uses a single Google/YouTube OAuth token that belongs to the channel
  owner only. It is used exclusively to upload the owner's own videos to their own
  YouTube channel via the YouTube Data API.
- No data is collected from viewers, subscribers, or any third party.
- No analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, or advertising identifiers are used by the
  automation itself.
- OAuth credentials (client id/secret and refresh token) are stored privately as
  encrypted repository secrets and are never published or shared.
- Video content is produced from public-domain and openly licensed sources
  (Quran text and recitations published openly, public-domain footage), with
  attribution where required.
